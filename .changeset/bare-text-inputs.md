Add variant="bare" to Input, Textarea, SearchInput, PasswordInput, NumberInput and InputGroup. Bare inputs render without a background, border, shadow, hover fill or container focus ring, while preserving size, padding, disabled state, validation semantics and action-button feedback. InputGroup and NumberInput also omit their internal dividers. Textarea keeps its row sizing and autosize behaviour when the border is removed.
