closeOnSelect, on the palette or on a single item, keeps the palette open after a selection: the search text is kept and focus returns to the input. loading shows a loading row, customised through the loading slot (Vue) or loadingContent (React), and the empty slot (Vue) or renderEmpty (React) replaces the empty content and receives the current search text. The loading and empty content now sit outside the listbox with role="status".
