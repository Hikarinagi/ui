Drop the radix-ui dependency. Dismissable layers, focus scopes, focus guards, presence, portals and collections now use Hina UI's own primitives with the same behavior as @hina-ui/vue: a tap outside an overlay dismisses it after the click instead of on pointer down, Escape is handled on window without preventing its default, and focus guards and toast items carry data-reka-focus-guard and data-reka-collection-item instead of their data-radix-* names.
