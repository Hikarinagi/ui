The heading slot (Vue) or renderHeading (React) replaces the content of a group heading row and receives the group, so a row can carry an action such as a clear button. The heading row is rendered outside the option list: its controls are reachable with Tab, clicking them leaves focus in the input, and the group keeps its label as its accessible name. It also works with virtualize.
