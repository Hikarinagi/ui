restoreKey now makes the shell save and restore the scroll position of its main area by itself: the position is kept in history.state per history entry, restored on reload, back and forward, and a new page starts at the top or at its #hash target. Server output carries an inline script that sets the position before hydration. createScrollRestoreSession and scrollRestoreScript are exported for scroll containers outside AppShell.
