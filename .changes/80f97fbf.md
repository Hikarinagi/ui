Add Tour, a step-by-step guide. A mask highlights each target and a card is placed next to it, or in the centre when a step has no target. Steps can be interactive, beforeChange can hold a step until async validation finishes, and the beacon mode opens each card from a pulsing dot. The content and footer can be replaced, arrow keys move between steps, Escape skips unless the tour is locked, and focus stays in the card and returns afterwards.
